# PhilaCon Valley

Philadelphia's tech community — by us, for us.

We're building a community-first tech ecosystem in Philadelphia: free and low-cost workshops, open-source tools, and events that connect builders across the city. PhilaCon Valley operates as two entities — a nonprofit running community programs and R&D workshops, and PhilaCon Valley, LLC, our agency arm, which packages proven solutions, development, and content for corporate clients.
