# Contributing to PhilaCon Valley projects

Thank you for wanting to help. This guide covers every PhilaCon Valley repository. A repository can
have its own `CONTRIBUTING.md` with extra steps. If it does, follow that one.

You do not need to be an expert. A typo fix, a clearer sentence, or a good bug report all count.

## Ways to help

- **Not on GitHub? Say it in Discord.** Most of the community lives in
  [Discord](https://discord.gg/5haHYh5xcx). Tell us there, and a team member turns it into an issue.
- **Report a bug.** Open an issue. Say what you did, what you expected, and what happened.
- **Suggest an idea.** Open an issue before you write a lot of code. We can agree on the plan first,
  so your work does not go to waste.
- **Send a fix.** Fork the repository, make a branch, and open a pull request.

## What makes a good pull request

- **One change per pull request.** Small pull requests get reviewed fast. Big ones wait.
- **Say why, not only what.** The diff shows what changed. Your description tells us why.
- **Link the issue** if there is one: `Fixes #12`.
- **Test it yourself first.** Run the project and check that your change works.
- **Show it.** For anything visual, add a screenshot or a short screen recording.

## What happens next

A maintainer reviews your pull request. We may ask questions or ask for changes. That is normal. It
is how we keep the projects working for everyone, not a sign that your work is wrong.

We review in our free time. If you hear nothing for a week, leave a comment on the pull request, or
ask in Discord.

## What we will not merge

- Changes that break the project for people who use it today.
- Large changes that nobody agreed to in an issue first.
- New dependencies or services when the project works without them.
- Code you cannot explain. You may use AI tools, but you must understand what you submit.
- Anything that breaks the [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/philaconvalley/community-handbook/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
